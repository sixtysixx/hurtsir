Injection is the step where order and timing matter most. Roblox has to be up and fully loaded
before the bootstrapper can attach, and the bootstrapper has to run with elevation or it won't
be able to touch the client.

Work through [Preparation & Prerequisites](/guide/preparation/) and
[Roblox Settings](/guide/roblox-settings/) first -most "injection failed" reports trace back to
something on one of those two pages.

## Step 1 - Launch Roblox and join a game

<Steps>

1. Launch Roblox **first**, before the bootstrapper.

2. Join any game. You can't inject from the Roblox home screen -there needs to be a live game
   client to attach to.

3. Let the game finish loading. If you inject while the loading screen is still up, the injector
   can attach to a client that isn't ready yet.

4. Leave Roblox in the foreground for the next step rather than minimising it.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
  Picking a small, private or empty game makes this much easier -there's less
  going on during startup, so "the game has finished loading" is unambiguous.
</Aside>

## Step 2 - Wait before injecting

<Steps>

1. After joining, wait **5-10 seconds** without doing anything else.

2. This gives the client time to finish initialising and for Roblox's own startup scripts to
   settle.

3. If you're on a slow machine or a large game, err on the longer side.

</Steps>

## Step 3 - Run the bootstrapper as Administrator

<Steps>

1. Locate the SirHurt Bootstrapper in the folder you set up during
   [Preparation](/guide/preparation/).

2. Right-click it and choose **Run as administrator**. Don't just double-click it -without
   elevation it can't attach to Roblox or write to the Roblox directory.

3. Approve the Windows UAC prompt.

4. If your antivirus pops up with a warning at this point, something in step 2 of
   [Preparation](/guide/preparation/) didn't stick. Fix the exclusions before going further.

</Steps>

## Step 4 - Confirm the bootstrapper sees Roblox

<Steps>

1. Leave the bootstrapper window open and let it settle.

2. It should report that it has located the running Roblox client. Wait for that status rather
   than clicking straight away.

3. If it sits at "waiting" or never detects Roblox, stop here and check
   [Troubleshooting](/guide/troubleshooting/) -injecting from that state is what produces
   "nothing happened".

</Steps>

## Step 5 - Inject

<Steps>

1. Click **Inject** once.

2. Log in when the prompt appears, using your SirHurt credentials.

3. Wait for it to report a successful attach.

</Steps>

<Aside type="danger" title="Do NOT click Inject a second time">
  Once you've logged in, the bootstrapper finishes the injection on its own.
  Clicking **Inject** again afterwards is one of the most common causes of a
  failed or half-finished injection, and it can leave you needing to restart
  Roblox before it works again.
</Aside>

## Step 6 - Confirm it actually works

Don't judge the injection by whether the UI looks right.

<Steps>

1. Pick a small, well-maintained test script such as **Infinite Yield**.

2. Execute it and watch for its console or on-screen output.

3. A script that runs confirms the injector is live. If it doesn't run, the problem is in the
   injection rather than the script -see [Troubleshooting](/guide/troubleshooting/).

4. Only test your real scripts once the injector is confirmed working, so you're not debugging
   two things at once.

</Steps>

## If it fails

<Steps>

1. Confirm the Roblox settings are still off. Roblox can reset them after an update.

2. Fully close Roblox, including any tray process, then start again from step 1.

3. If the bootstrapper hangs or closes instantly, use the **Kill Processes** button in the UI
   and retry.

4. Work the matching symptom on the [Troubleshooting](/guide/troubleshooting/) page instead of
   guessing -each one is a numbered walkthrough.

5. If you're told you're on an unsupported version, that's a Roblox update, not a broken setup.
   See the [Downgrade Tutorial](/guide/downgrade/).

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Still stuck?">
  Collect your Windows version (`winver`), the Roblox build hash and the full
  bootstrapper log, then post them in our [Discord](https://discord.gg/sirhurt).
  Those three details turn "it doesn't work" into something that can actually be
  diagnosed.
</Aside>
